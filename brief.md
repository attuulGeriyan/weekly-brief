# Marketing Brief: Week 12 Performance Review

Blended spend increased in the latest week, but conversions and revenue declined, leading to a higher blended CPA that missed targets. The TikTok budget increase experiment exceeded its CPA threshold and requires a decision.

## Last week vs the period
Blended spend in week 12 was $23,030, 10.8% higher than the $20,785.27 weekly average over weeks 1-11. Blended conversions (288) and revenue ($17,856) were both 1.7% lower than their respective weekly averages.

## What the numbers mean
Blended CPA in week 12 was $79.97, missing the Q3 target of under $65 (note_04_targets). Blended revenue of $17,856 also missed its Q3 target of above $25k (note_04_targets). The Q3 target period is incomplete, ending Sep 28 for a Sep 30 deadline (note_04_targets). TikTok spend averaged $4,787.83 per week over weeks 7-12, 66.7% higher than the $2,872.33 baseline, following a budget increase (note_02_tiktok). The TikTok CPA ratio to Meta CPA was 1.92x over weeks 7-12, exceeding the 1.5x (note_02_tiktok) threshold. Meta spend averaged $8,935.50 per week over weeks 9-12, 26.9% higher than the $7,040.38 baseline, due to a brand awareness campaign (note_03_brand). Do not judge this campaign on direct CPA (note_03_brand). Google Search CPA in week 6 was $202.90, 222.1% higher than the $62.99 baseline. Google Ads conversion tracking was broken Aug 7-13 (note_01_tracking), making conversions for weeks 5-6 invalid and CPA uncertain (note_01_tracking). Email CPA in week 8 was $23.25, 35.7% higher than the $17.14 baseline.

## Decisions needed
Decide whether to cut the TikTok budget increase experiment, as its CPA ratio exceeded the agreed threshold.

## Recommended actions
- Cut the TikTok budget increase experiment; TikTok's CPA ratio exceeded the 1.5x (note_02_tiktok) threshold against Meta CPA.
- Review blended CPA and revenue strategy to address missed Q3 targets.
- Investigate the cause of the Email CPA increase in week 8.

## Data caveats
Google Ads conversion tracking was broken from Aug 7-13 (note_01_tracking), affecting Google Search CPA in weeks 5-6 (note_01_tracking). Meta's brand awareness campaign in week 9 should not be judged on direct CPA (note_03_brand). Blended Q3 CPA and revenue targets are based on an incomplete period, ending Sep 28 for a Sep 30 deadline (note_04_targets).
