# Weekly Marketing Brief - Week 12

Overall blended performance declined in the latest week, with CPA increasing and ROAS decreasing. We need to address the underperforming TikTok experiment and the blended CPA target miss.

## Last week vs the period
Blended spend in week 12 was $23,030, 10.8% higher than the $20,785.27 baseline from weeks 1-11. Blended CPA in week 12 was $79.97, 12.7% higher than the $70.94 baseline from weeks 1-11. Blended ROAS in week 12 was 0.78x, 10.3% lower than the 0.87x baseline from weeks 1-11.

## What the numbers mean
Google Search CPA in week 6 was $202.90, 222.1% higher than the $62.99 baseline. However, Google Ads conversion data for weeks 5 and 6 (Aug 7-13) is invalid due to a broken conversion tag (note_01_tracking); weekly data cannot isolate affected days, so these records are uncertain.

TikTok spend in weeks 7-12 was $4,787.83, 66.7% higher than the $2,872.33 baseline, due to the 1.6x (note_02_tiktok) budget increase for the experiment. The TikTok CPA to Meta CPA ratio over weeks 7-12 was 1.92x, exceeding the 1.5x (note_02_tiktok) cut-off threshold (note_02_tiktok).

Meta conversions in weeks 8-12 were 107, 23.0% higher than the 87 baseline. This period includes a brand awareness campaign (note_03_brand); do not judge it on direct CPA (note_03_brand).

Email CPA in week 8 was $23.25, 35.7% higher than the $17.14 baseline.

Blended CPA in week 12 was $79.97, which did not meet the Q3 target of under $65 (note_04_targets).

## Decisions needed
Decision on the TikTok experiment based on the CPA ratio exceeding the threshold.

## Recommended actions
- Review TikTok campaign performance and consider adjusting budget or pausing the experiment, as its CPA ratio of 1.92x exceeds the 1.5x (note_02_tiktok) threshold for cutting (note_02_tiktok).
- Investigate the blended CPA increase in week 12 to identify contributing factors and develop a plan to meet the Q3 target of under $65 (note_04_targets).
- Monitor Meta campaign performance, acknowledging the brand awareness campaign's impact on last-click conversions (note_03_brand).

## Data caveats
Google Ads conversion data for weeks 5 and 6 (Aug 7-13) is invalid due to a broken conversion tag (note_01_tracking), affecting Google Search CPA. Weekly data cannot isolate affected days, so those records are uncertain.
